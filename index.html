Why only 1-3 photos per look?
Because a folder of 40 near-duplicates is what you already tried. Every photo we hand you is a different version of you — edited, sequenced, ready to post. Quality of looks over quantity of files is the product. The only way to get more photos is to buy more looks, not more edits of the same one.
What if I'm not photogenic?
Nobody is photogenic unprompted. Models are directed. The Shot Map decides every pose and angle before shoot day — awkwardness is a planning failure, not a face failure. You'll never stand there wondering what to do with your hands.
