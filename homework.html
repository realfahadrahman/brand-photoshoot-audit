📋 How the audit call works

  • ✅ 30 minutes, via phone or video (your choice)
  • ✅ We review your photos across every platform live
  • ✅ We define your archetype together
  • ✅ We map out your looks and give you a posting plan
  • ✅ You leave with a written audit report within 24h after the call

📖 Before the call, please review these 3 concepts

1. Looks, not photos. The unit of a personal brand is the look: one location × one outfit. Three planned looks gives you a month of content. Fifty pics from one afternoon gives you one day. We'll explain why this matters on the call.

2. Direction beats genetics. Nobody is photogenic unprompted. Models get directed. Every pose and angle is decided before the shoot. Awkwardness is a planning failure, not a face failure.

3. The profile is a system. Your photos work together or they don't work at all. The order matters. The bio matters. The first photo matters most. We'll rebuild your whole profile as one system on the call.
