The Presentation

This is the deck Cole said he would draft on 2026-08-19, structured. It follows Brunson's Perfect Webinar compressed to the 15 minute version: one Big Domino, three belief breaks, then the stack with re-stacking after every element. It runs three ways with the same slides: shared on a sales call after the probe, sent as a link after a DM conversation, or delivered live to a small group. Presentation rule that drives everything: teach the what, never the how. The how is the product.

The Big Domino (write this first, sell only this)

If they believe that a directed looks system, not more edited pics, is the key to finally looking online the way they look in person, and that it is only attainable through our archetype-driven process, then every other objection stops mattering.

Every slide either strikes this domino or gets cut.

Part 1 — Open (slides 1 to 5, ~3 minutes)

  1. Title: "How to look like the main character online in one afternoon, without being photogenic, rich, or a model."
  2. It's not your fault. "You've probably tried: the friend with a camera, the 400 selfies, maybe even a real photographer. You got a folder of pics and nothing changed. Nobody told you the folder was never the product." (Justify failures, allay fears, throw a rock at the enemy: photographers who count pics.)
  3. Who this is for: dating profile, IG, LinkedIn, founder brand. All of them, one system. (Inclusive so nobody opts out mentally.)
  4. The promise: "By the end of this you'll know exactly why your photos haven't worked and the exact system that fixes it in one afternoon."
  5. Origin story, 60 seconds: the epiphany that photos fail as a pile and work as a system. Told as story, not claim: the guy who asked us for "25 edited pics" and the realization that he was asking for the wrong unit entirely.

Part 2 — The Three Secrets (slides 6 to 14, ~7 minutes)

Each secret: state it, micro-story, other-people proof, rapid belief breaks ("you're probably thinking X, right? Actually..."), restate as truth. Trial close every slide.

Secret 1, the vehicle: "Looks, not pics."

  • The unit of a personal brand is the look: location x outfit. 50 edited pics of two outfits is two looks wearing a costume of abundance.
  • Belief break: "You're probably thinking more photos = more content. Actually three planned looks outperform fifty pics because every look is a different day of your life. Fifty pics from one afternoon in one fit is one day, posted fifty times."
  • Restate: you don't have a photo problem, you have a looks problem.

Secret 2, internal: "Direction beats genetics."

  • Belief to break: "I'm not photogenic."
  • Truth: nobody is photogenic unprompted. Models are directed. The Shot Map decides every pose and angle before shoot day; awkwardness is a planning failure, not a face failure.
  • Proof: before and afters of ordinary clients (Bonus 2 in offer.md exists to farm these).

Secret 3, external: "You don't need a life upgrade first."

  • Belief to break: "I need the physique / wardrobe / lifestyle before a shoot is worth it."
  • Truth: the Prep Protocol peaks what you already have in two weeks, the Archetype Session picks fits you own or can assemble cheaply, and three ordinary Montreal locations read as a full life when the looks are planned. The shoot is the shortcut to looking like the guy who has his life together, not the reward for it.

Recap as an if-then chain: "If the unit is looks not pics, and direction beats genetics, and you don't need a life upgrade first, is there any reason your profile can't be fixed in one afternoon?" Nodding = domino down.

Part 3 — The Stack (slides 15 to 25, ~5 minutes)

Permission pivot first: "Can I take five minutes and show you exactly what working with us looks like?"

Reveal one element per slide, then re-show the cumulative stack after every element so the last thing seen before the price is always the full offer:

  • Slide 15: Archetype Discovery Session (C$250) → stack
  • Slide 16: The Prep Protocol (C$200) → stack
  • Slide 17: Your Shot Map (C$150) → stack
  • Slide 18: The Shoot, 3 looks across up to 3 locations (C$1,200) → stack
  • Slide 19: Pro edits on every final, 1 to 3 per look, all postable (C$300) → stack
  • Slide 20: 30-Day Posting Map (C$300) → stack
  • Slide 21: Profile Teardown (C$200) → full stack, total C$2,600
  • Slide 22: If/all closes, one per secret: "If all this did was give you three different-day looks, worth it? If all it did was make you comfortable in front of a camera for life? If all it did was give you a month of presence without changing anything about your life?"
  • Slide 23: Price. "You'd expect C$2,600. It's C$1,500." CTA appears here and stays on every slide after.
  • Slide 24: Guarantee: reshoot a full look free if you wouldn't proudly post the floor count. Two-choices close: keep the current profile, or test this with the risk on us.
  • Slide 25: Honest urgency: founding pricing plus the free extra look ends at five clients, N shoots per month. Book the call / pay the deposit.

Rules for whoever presents this

  • Never skip re-stacking. The stack slide count feels repetitive to the presenter and decisive to the buyer.
  • Never deep-dive the how of any element. Overwhelm kills sales and "educated" prospects leave to do it themselves, badly.
  • All claims stay inside what we control. No promised matches, followers, or clients.
  • Case study slides get swapped in as real ones exist; until then, use the founding-client framing honestly: "you'd be one of the five we build the proof wall with."
