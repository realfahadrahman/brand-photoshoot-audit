# The Grand Slam Offer

Built per [100M Offers](../../books/hormozi/100m-offers.md): raise dream outcome and certainty, crush time delay and effort, price premium, make it impossible to price-compare against a photographer. The category escape is already decided: **we sell a personal brand in image form, photographers sell edited pics.** The moment a prospect compares us to a photographer on price per photo, the positioning has failed.

## Who it is for

Men 20 to 40 who need to look like somebody online and know their current photos are costing them: dating results, Instagram presence, LinkedIn credibility, founder brand. They have money and no idea what to shoot, what to wear, or how to stand. The market check passes: pain is real and felt weekly, they can pay, they cluster (gyms, founder circles, dating app users), and personal branding is a growing market.

## The core mechanism: the Looks Formula

Decided 2026-08-19, recorded in [context.md](./context.md). This is the engine under every package.

- **1 look = 1 location x 1 outfit.** Outfit change in the same spot is a new look.
- **Each look yields 1 to 3 final edited photos, hard maximum 3.** Set by Fahad 2026-08-19. Scarcity per look is deliberate: the only way to get more photos is to buy more looks, which keeps the unit clean and the price premium.
- **Price = C$500 per look, two-look minimum, plus location premiums and extras.**
  - Per look (direction, shooting, edits): C$500
  - Location change premium when locations are far apart: C$20 to C$100 each
  - Extras (rush edits, extra retouch, video clips): priced individually, never bundled silently
- **Never quote edited-photo counts.** When a prospect asks "how much for 25 pics" (the exact inbound that started this), the answer is the reframe: "Depends what the 25 are for. Let me ask you three questions." Then the probe call. At 1 to 3 finals per look, 25 pics is a 9-plus look engagement, which is a custom quote north of C$4,500, and the probe call exists to convert that raw-count request into the package he actually needs.

## The packages

Three defined, but **only one is quoted on any given call**, chosen from the probe call. A menu creates comparison shopping inside your own offer. Good/Better/Best exists for anchoring on the presentation, not as a pick-your-own buffet.

| Package | Contents | Final photos | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Profile Fix** | 2 looks, 1 to 2 locations | 2 to 6 | **C$1,000** |
| **The Main Character** (flagship) | 3 looks, up to 3 locations, full stack below | 3 to 9 | **C$1,500** |
| **The Full Rebrand** | 6 looks plus 1 bonus look free, up to 4 locations | 7 to 21 | **C$3,000** |

The flagship is the offer. The Profile Fix exists to be downsold into, never led with. The Full Rebrand is the upsell for founders and anyone rebuilding everything at once; the seventh look is a bonus, not a discount, because volume buys more value, never a lower rate.

**Group option (pending confirmation of the "2-3 people idea"):** 2 to 3 clients share a session block, each gets a reduced package at roughly 70 percent of solo price. Raises revenue per shoot hour, and the clients recruit each other. Do not build this page until the note's intent is confirmed with Cole.

## The stack (present in this order, re-stack after each element)

Anchored per Hormozi. Every element kills one specific reason the dream outcome fails.

| # | Element | What the client hears | Kills | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Archetype Discovery Session** | "Before anyone picks up a camera, we figure out who you are online. The frame decides everything: fits, locations, poses, captions." | "I don't know what my brand is" | C$250 |
| 2 | **The Prep Protocol** | "A two-week checklist so you show up at your visual peak: grooming, skin, haircut timing, and a physique peak-day protocol. You look the best you have ever looked, on schedule." | "I'm not photogenic / not ready" | C$200 |
| 3 | **Your Shot Map** | "A pre-planned shot list: every location, every fit, every pose, decided before shoot day. You never stand there wondering what to do with your hands." | awkwardness, effort | C$150 |
| 4 | **The Shoot: 3 looks across up to 3 locations** | "One afternoon. We direct everything. You just show up with the bag we told you to pack." | effort, time | C$1,200 |
| 5 | **Pro edits on every final** | "Skin, colour, light. Natural, never uncanny. Every photo you get is finished, there is no filler." | "edited pics look fake" | C$300 |
| 6 | **The 30-Day Posting Map** | "You leave with a month sequenced: what to post first, how each look stretches across feed, stories, dating profile and LinkedIn. Nine finals, thirty days of presence." | "then what?" | C$300 |
| 7 | **Profile Teardown** | "We rebuild your profile order and bio around the new shots. The photos work as a system, not a pile." | wasted assets | C$200 |

**Total anchored value: C$2,600. Price: C$1,500.**

The few-photos objection is answered inside the stack, never apologized for: every final is postable, and a folder of 40 near-duplicates is what they already tried. Quality of looks over quantity of files is the product.

## Bonuses

- **Bonus 1: First Post in 48 Hours.** One finished photo per look delivered within 48 hours of the shoot so the transformation goes live while the momentum is hot. This is the fast win that crushes time delay; everything else can take the normal edit window.
- **Bonus 2: The Founding Client Look.** One extra look free for the first five clients, in exchange for a before-and-after usage right. This builds the proof wall that replaces the bonus later.

## The guarantee

**"If you don't get at least one shot per look you would proudly post, we reshoot a full look free. You pick the location."**

Bounded and controllable: it promises work, not outcomes we do not control (matches, followers, clients). Never guarantee dating or business results. The Prep Protocol and Shot Map make a bad shoot nearly impossible, which is what makes this guarantee cheap.

## Scarcity and urgency, honest versions only

- **Scarcity:** Cole shoots a fixed number of sessions per month around his schedule. "We book [N] shoots a month. Next opening: [date]." True by construction, state the real N.
- **Urgency:** founding-client pricing and Bonus 2 end at five clients. After that the flagship goes to C$1,800 or higher as proof accumulates, same escalation logic as the web services ladder in [web-services/offer.md](../web-services/offer.md).

## The one-line pitch

> "One afternoon. Thirty days of content. You finally look online the way you're trying to look."

## Rules

- No per-photo pricing, ever. The unit is the look.
- Hard maximum of 3 finals per look. Handing over extras "because they turned out great" gives away the upsell.
- No quoting without the probe call. See [sales-call-script.md](./sales-call-script.md).
- No discounting the flagship. Downsell to a smaller package instead of a smaller price; upsell volume with bonus looks, never a lower rate.
- Extras are charged, not thrown in. Free extras teach clients that scope is negotiable.
- The "Coreycha rip and repackage" task from Fahad's note stays open: model the structure of that competitor's offer, keep the looks formula as the engine.
