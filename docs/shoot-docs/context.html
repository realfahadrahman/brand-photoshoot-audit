Personal Brand Shoots — Context

What it is: a personal branding photography service run with Cole, selling done-for-you photo systems to men building an online presence (Instagram, dating profiles, LinkedIn, founder brands). The product is not photos. The product is a personal brand in image form.

Origin, recorded 2026-08-19: inbound demand arrived before the offer existed. A prospect messaged Fahad: "Yo question. Can I get separate pricing from ur marketing agency for 25 and 50 edited pics." Cole and Fahad decided the same day to package it properly rather than quote ad hoc. Cole's framing from that conversation is the positioning line: "We not selling photos, we selling personal branding."

Decisions already made (from the 2026-08-19 conversation)

Do not reopen these without new information.

  1. The pricing unit is the "look", not the edited photo count. A look = location x outfit. Three shots of the same outfit in the same spot is one look, not three photos. Photographers price on edited-shot counts; that framing was explicitly rejected ("fk the photographers, they're not business people").
  2. Final edited photos are 1 to 3 per look, hard maximum 3. Set by Fahad 2026-08-19 (afternoon texts to Cole floated 1 to 2; settled at 1 to 3 the same day). Supersedes Cole's looser "2 to 3 poses or angles" range. The cap is deliberate: more photos means buying more looks.
  3. An outfit change counts as a new look even in the same location. For IG branding and dating, what matters is postable pictures, not geography.
  4. Distant location changes are a premium, roughly C$20 to C$100 per location change, not a separate package.
  5. The formula comes first, extras are add-ons. Price = f(looks) + location premiums + extras. Anything not in the formula is an extra charge.
  6. A probe call precedes any quote. Cole: "Also need to figure out what they want exactly with a call. But have an outline before going into it." The outline is offer.md; the call is sales-call-script.md.
