# The Offer: Free Personal Brand Photo Audit

## Core Concept

A free 30-minute audit call where we review a prospect's photos across every platform, define their archetype, map out their looks, and give them a 30-day posting plan. The audit is the funnel entry point — the paid shoot gets sold on the call.

## Why Free?

- **Lowest friction possible.** No credit card, no commitment. The goal is volume of qualified leads.
- **Value-first.** They get real deliverables (6 of them) for free. Reciprocity drives shoot bookings.
- **Qualification on the call.** We screen for fit during the audit — not via a form.
- **No price on the page.** Value is built on the call, not the landing page.

## The 6 Deliverables (Given Free)

1. **Written Audit Report** — Every platform, every photo, every gap, every fix. Personalized.
2. **Defined Archetype** — The identity that drives every visual decision (athlete, creative, founder, etc.)
3. **Custom Looks Map** — What to shoot, what to wear, where. Numbered and sequenced.
4. **The Prep Protocol** — Two-week checklist to peak appearance for shoot day (grooming, skin, haircut timing, physique).
5. **30-Day Posting Roadmap** — Day-by-day content calendar across all platforms.
6. **Profile Rebuild Guide** — Exact photo order, bio copy, and system-level profile design.

## The Funnel

```
Landing Page → Free Audit Form → Homework Page → Audit Call (30 min) → Shoot Booking
```

1. **Landing page** (`index.html`) — sells the free audit, not the shoot
2. **Form submission** — captures name, email, Instagram, primary platform, #1 struggle
3. **Homework page** (`homework.html`) — 3-step pre-call questionnaire (goals/platforms, style/archetype, logistics)
4. **Audit call** — 30 min, personalized, working session not a pitch
5. **Shoot booking** — sold on the call based on value established

## Pricing (Not Public)

Pricing is discussed on the call only. The landing page has zero price mentions by design.

## Scarcity Mechanism

- **10 audit calls per month** — stated on landing page
- **Live slot counter** — currently simulated via JS, will be replaced with real availability data
- Creates urgency without being pushy

## Target Audience

- Men 18-35 primarily
- Active on Instagram, dating apps, LinkedIn, or building a personal brand
- Frustrated with how they look in photos vs. real life
- Have tried: friend with a camera, selfies, paid photographer, or "waiting until ready"

## Geographic Scope

- Audit calls: remote, anywhere
- Shoots: Montreal-based (stated on landing page)
- Three ordinary Montreal locations read as a "full, varied life" when looks are planned
