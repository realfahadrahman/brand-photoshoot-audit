# Copy Frameworks Used

## Landing Page Structure: Emotion → Logic → Fear

The page follows a 3-block narrative arc:

### Block 1: EMOTION
**Hero → Problem → Agitation → Transformation**

- **Hero:** "You look good in person. Your photos don't show it." — hits the core pain instantly
- **Problem:** 4 problem cards (friend with camera, selfie spiral, waiting to be ready, paid photographer who just handed over files)
- **Agitation banner:** "Every day your profile stays like this, you're losing matches, credibility, and opportunities"
- **Transformation:** Before/After comparison — same person, same phone, completely different presence

### Block 2: LOGIC
**Solution → Value Stack → Credibility**

- **Solution:** 6-step system (profile teardown, archetype definition, looks mapping, prep protocol, posting roadmap, profile rebuild)
- **Value stack:** 6 deliverables spelled out as tangible assets they keep
- **Credibility:** Stats block (6 deliverables, 3 looks beats 50 pics, 30min call, $0 cost)

### Block 3: FEAR
**Urgency → Application**

- **Scarcity:** 10 audit calls/month, live slot counter
- **Application:** Audit form (not just "sign up" — it's "book a call")

## Frameworks Applied

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
- Problem: photos don't match reality
- Agitate: losing matches, credibility, opportunities daily
- Solution: the audit system

### StoryBrand (Donald Miller)
- Hero: the prospect
- Guide: us (we've been where you are)
- Problem: photos not working
- Plan: the 6-step audit system
- Success: profile that matches who you are in person
- Failure: staying invisible, losing opportunities

### SUCCESs (Made to Stick)
- **Simple:** "You look good in person. Your photos don't show it."
- **Unexpected:** "A photoshoot was never the answer. A system is."
- **Concrete:** "Three planned looks across three locations gives you a month of content."
- **Credible:** "Models are directed. Nobody is photogenic unprompted."
- **Emotional:** "Stop guessing. Start looking like yourself."
- **Stories:** Problem cards use relatable scenarios (friend with camera, 400 selfies, etc.)

## Key Copy Decisions

- **No prices anywhere** — value built on the call
- **"Looks, not photos"** as the core mental model shift
- **"Direction beats genetics"** to defuse "I'm not photogenic" objection
- **"The profile is a system"** to elevate from "take better selfies" to "build a presence"
- **Application framing** — "Book Your Free Photo Audit" not "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
